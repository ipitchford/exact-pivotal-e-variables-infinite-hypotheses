# Prior-art record: exact pivotal e-variables for infinite hypotheses (15 November 2026)

Sources read for positioning (relevant sections in full):
- Zhang, Ramdas and Wang, Ann. Statist. 52(5):2241-2267, 2024, doi:10.1214/24-AOS2434 (print version and arXiv:2305.16539v4).
  Used: Theorem 6.1 (finite existence, joint atomlessness), Proposition 3.3, Proposition 6.4, Theorem 6.8 (bounded exact criterion,
  tightness sentence), Conjecture 6.9, Example 6.11 (sign test: binary exact e-value and randomised exact p-value), Corollary 7.1;
  arXiv Example A.4. Print numbering used; arXiv v4 numbers 6.7, 6.8, 6.10 noted.
- Ramdas and Wang, Hypothesis testing with e-values, arXiv:2410.23614v6: Sections 3.1-3.2 (test randomisation; Theorem 3.9),
  Example 3.15, Section 14.3 / Theorem 14.6 ("requires modern tools from optimal transport").
- Ram, Larsson, Ruf and Ramdas, arXiv:2606.24768v2: Example 5.1 (exact unbounded e-variable with uniform positive e-power, null in
  the closed alternative hull), Theorem 4.17 (compactness and truncation).
- Larsson, Ruf and Ramdas, arXiv:2601.05217v3: Theorem 1.8 (narrow compactness of both convex families).
- He and Cheng, arXiv:2609.27923v1: Theorem 2.2 (finite nulls, n observations, exact pivotal rate tends to min_i D(Q||P_i)).
- Forre, arXiv:2609.27765v1 and 2609.27766v1 (e-power versus finite-horizon detection).
- Ramdas, arXiv:2609.05752v1: cited only for the content of its abstract (power-one sequential testability for i.i.d. models).
- Classical background cited for standard material: Lehmann-Romano (similar tests), Le Cam (Kraft-Le Cam separation), Blackwell 1953
  (comparison of experiments), Kelly 1956, Albiac-Kalton (Dunford-Pettis, Krein-Smulian), Vovk-Wang 2021, Grunwald-de Heide-Koolen 2024.

Attribution decisions: the binary randomisation, the similar-test reading and the Kelly bet are classical; the refutation of the
"only if" direction of Conjecture 6.9 for unbounded e-variables is prior work (Ramdas-Wang Example 3.15; Ram-Larsson-Ruf-Ramdas
Example 5.1). The candidate contributions are: the infinite-family sufficiency with the I(delta) guarantee and its optimality in delta;
the transport-free route to Theorem 6.1 (e)=>(a),(b); the closed-span obstruction for pivotal statistics and the example where
pivotality removes all e-power; the counterexample to the tightness sentence and the weak-compactness repair; the compact case of
Conjecture 6.9. Searches (arXiv, Semantic Scholar, citing literature of ZRW) found no earlier statement of these results; this is a
record of the searches made, not a claim of historical priority.
